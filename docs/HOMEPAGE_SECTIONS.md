# Home hierarchy

1. Full photograph behind a dark overlay. Centered tagline and two-line headline. A scroll cue opens the story; no crowded hero card grid.
2. Business discovery: a short problem statement next to a restrained, interactive journey.
3. Quiet process: understand, agree priorities, build and use. Link to the full Services process.
4. Everyday digitalization: notebook illustration next to one selected website, transaction or report example. Only one visual panel appears at a time.
5. After-sales: a calm copy section explaining adoption and continued care.
6. Real work: one large screenshot and concise story at a time. Published featured projects follow `order`; initially Poseidon Filter and Leaf Lab. Link to the full portfolio.
7. Closing invitation: a clear route to Contact.

Products have their own directory and detailed use-case pages. Technology context stays practical and evergreen, with no fabricated statistics or decorative market charts. The same hierarchy is used in ID and EN. Mobile stacks each visual with its context and preserves the full-photo opening.
