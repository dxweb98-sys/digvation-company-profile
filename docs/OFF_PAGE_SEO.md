# Off-page SEO readiness

The codebase can make pages crawlable, shareable, and measurable, but it cannot create authority by itself. The strongest current organic assets are solution pages, real case studies, shareable project URLs, structured metadata, and accurate business/social configuration.

Useful external work after launch:

- maintain an accurate Google Business Profile if Digvation has an eligible real business presence;
- keep NAP information consistent across legitimate business citations;
- ask approved clients for links to relevant case studies;
- distribute solution guides, case-study material, demos, and original diagrams through relevant professional channels;
- build partner/referral links where a genuine working relationship exists;
- pursue relevant editorial or industry coverage when there is real expertise or news to contribute;
- use paid search separately from SEO and track conversions through GA4/Google Ads.

Avoid link farms, mass directory spam, purchased fake reviews, and fabricated location pages.
