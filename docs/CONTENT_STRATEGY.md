# Content strategy

Digvation is a digitalization partner. The founder confirms Digvation began in 2022, helping small and medium businesses. Explain the work through needs people recognize: a clear website, organized everyday work, useful products, and help after launch. The public tagline stays “Partner digital kalian.”

The About story follows three moments: the first step in 2022; today's effort to become a useful ongoing partner; and the ambition to support larger businesses as capabilities and reputation grow. Enterprise work is an aspiration, not an invented current client claim. Do not manufacture project counts, awards, team size, locations, testimonials, outcomes, timelines or response-time promises.

Use short, direct, human Indonesian and equivalent natural English. Avoid inflated innovation claims, empty adjective lists, jargon-heavy technology headlines and em dashes in public copy. Explain what a business can do next. The full-image hero contains the tagline and centered headline; detailed information starts below it.

Poseidon Filter and Leaf Lab are the actual portfolio evidence. Their screenshots, live URLs and qualitative case-study facts come from the original repository. Interactive notebooks, POS and Workshop surfaces contain illustrative sample data and are explicitly labeled. Show business context before implementation capabilities.

Home alternates visual explanation and quieter sections. Deeper information belongs on Services, Work/details, Products/details, About, Contact and legal pages. Contact explains what happens next and requests only the context needed to begin. After-sales means adoption, maintenance and future improvement are discussed as part of scope; do not promise unlimited support or an unapproved SLA.
